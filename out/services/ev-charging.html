FAQs

Common questions before installation

Can EV charging work with rooftop solar?

Yes. Solar arrays can offset part or most of the charging energy load depending on site configuration and daytime usage.

Do you supply both AC and DC chargers?

Yes. We recommend the right charger type based on expected usage, power availability and user requirements.
